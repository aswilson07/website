Research

Dr. Ashia Wilson runs the Optimization, Safety, and Evaluation (OSE) Lab. The lab develops methods and foundations for AI safety, focusing on image-based abuse and child safety, and on understanding and mitigating epistemic harms such as homogenization and sycophancy.
