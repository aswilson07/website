Research

Dr. Ashia Wilson runs the Optimization, Safety, and Evaluation (OSE) Lab. The lab develops the foundations of optimization, designing efficient optimization and sampling algorithms for modern machine learning. It develops methods and foundations for AI safety, with a focus on child safety and understanding and mitigating epistemic harms such as homogenization and sycophancy. It also develops methods and foundations for evaluating AI systems to better understand, measure, and improve their capabilities, limitations, and societal impacts.
