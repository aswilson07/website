Research

Dr. Ashia Wilson runs the Optimization, Safety, and Evaluation (OSE) Lab. The lab develops foundations for machine learning systems that are efficient, safe, and meaningfully evaluated. Our work spans optimization and sampling methods for efficient and reliable learning at scale; AI safety, with a focus on image-based abuse, child safety, and epistemic harms such as homogenization and sycophancy; and evaluation methods that support valid, decision-relevant conclusions about AI systems and their societal impacts.
