Academic bio (short)

Ashia C. Wilson is the Lister Brothers Career Development Associate Professor in MIT EECS. She runs the Optimization, Safety, and Evaluation (OSE) Lab, which studies machine learning evaluation, algorithmic fairness, privacy and unlearning, optimization, and sampling.

Academic bio (long)

Dr. Ashia Wilson is the Lister Brothers Career Development Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at MIT. She is affiliated with LIDS, the Statistics and Data Science Center, and MIT AI for Society.

She runs the Optimization, Safety, and Evaluation (OSE) Lab. Her research develops mathematical and statistical tools for building and assessing machine learning systems, with work spanning evaluation methodology, algorithmic fairness and homogenization, privacy-preserving learning and unlearning, and optimization and sampling algorithms.

Dr. Wilson earned a B.A. from Harvard, concentrating in applied mathematics with a minor in philosophy, and a Ph.D. in statistics from UC Berkeley. Before joining MIT, she was a postdoctoral researcher in the machine learning and statistics group at Microsoft Research.
