Postdocs

Exceptional postdoctoral researchers with aligned interests in evaluation, safety, optimization are welcome to reach out with a CV, research statement, and a brief note describing fit.

Graduate students applying to MIT

MIT graduate admissions are handled by department committees. If your interests align with the lab, please do not email me, but describe that fit in your application materials.

Current MIT students

Current MIT students interested in research should read the research page, identify a concrete area of overlap, and email a brief note with background, interests, and availability.
